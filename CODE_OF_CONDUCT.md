# Code of Conduct

This project is for families, friends, teachers, and anyone who wants to make games together. Everyone who takes part should be treated with respect.

## Our standards

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to programming or to this project.
- Assume good intent. Ask questions before you assume a mistake was deliberate.
- Keep feedback about the work, not the person.
- Keep people's private information private. Never share someone's photos, names or answers from a game without their permission.
- Keep content suitable for a family audience, including game names, questions and write-ins.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats or repeated unwanted contact.
- Discrimination based on who someone is.
- Posting someone else's private information.
- Content that is sexual, violent or hateful, or that targets a person.

## Reporting a problem

Report concerns by opening a GitHub issue marked "conduct", or, for anything you would rather not post publicly, by contacting the repository owner through their GitHub profile. You do not need to prove what happened. Describe what you saw and when.

The maintainer will read every report and respond as soon as they can. They may remove content, ask someone to change their behaviour, or ask someone to leave the project. Anyone who is asked to stop must do so.

## Scope

This applies in every space the project uses: issues, pull requests, discussions, and games shared with the project.
